THE QUEENS EXPERIENCE

Small salon.
Personal attention.

Queens Beauty Palace operates as a home-based salon in the Montreal Spring / Wellington Circle area. Public customer feedback consistently highlights friendly service, reasonable pricing and a no-hard-selling approach.

Neighbourhood feel

The setting is intentionally more personal than a large commercial salon. Advance contact is recommended so the salon can prepare for your appointment and confirm access.

Advice first

Not sure whether you need colour, a perm, rebonding or simply a cut? Describe your current hair and desired result when you message. A consultation can help avoid choosing the wrong treatment.

Value-focused

Queens Beauty Palace has been featured in Singapore affordable-salon guides for its accessible neighbourhood positioning. Published prices can change, so current rates should always be confirmed directly.

More than hair

Public business listings associate the salon with manicure and pedicure services, and local profiles also mention eyebrow embroidery and eyelash services. Confirm which services are currently offered.

BEFORE YOU ARRIVE

One message.
Clear appointment.

Because this is a home-based salon, contact the salon before travelling. Confirm the service, price, appointment time and any access instructions.
