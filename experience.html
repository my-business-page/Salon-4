THE ARTISTS

People behind
the craft.

Suchehwa’s public team profiles show a deep bench across Korean trends, creative colour and advanced perming.

Ted Kim · Managing Director

Ted honed his craft in Gangnam, Seoul. He is known for Korean trends, signature perms and creating styles clients can reproduce at home.

Gene Niu · Creative Director

Gene has 27 years in hairdressing and credentials including Hoyu Professional Colour Master, Revlon Master of Colour and Shiseido Beauty Innovator. He specialises in creative colour, Korean perms and Japanese/Korean/international trends.

Von Seah · Director

Von has more than 22 years of experience and specialises in perming across Asian and Caucasian hair types, with a strong eye for personalised transformation.

Kazu · Director

Kazu is described as a master in hair design with experience in leading Singapore salons and a focus on personalised design.
